19917 145th St E · Tehaleh

Over $100K in upgrades and a rare combination of designer interiors, generous outdoor living and a peaceful greenbelt setting!

This beautifully updated Tehaleh home in the Sumner-Bonney Lake School District impresses from the moment you enter. A soaring two-story great room with fireplace opens to a gorgeous kitchen with oversized island, white shaker cabinetry, brass hardware, gas range and walk-in pantry. Custom millwork, designer accents and updated lighting add character throughout. Main-floor 5th bedroom/office (no closet) beside a ¾ bath, plus a beautifully appointed mudroom. Upstairs, the primary suite enjoys Mount Rainier views, soaking tub, frameless glass shower and custom closet system. Two more bedrooms also capture mountain views, while an oversized fourth bedroom with walk-in closet overlooks the greenbelt and doubles as a potential bonus room.

A full bath, laundry with quartz counters and sink, and new flooring complete the upper level. A generous fenced lot backing to recorded open-space greenbelt and towering evergreens. The new 24×22 covered outdoor living space with composite decking and a plumbed fire pit, with a large covered storage closet beside the deck. Central AC, Cat-6 wiring, EV charger and an extra-long garage with kick-out storage. Enjoy Tehaleh’s 40+ miles of trails, 18 parks, Post & Pour café and the planned Retreat clubhouse (opening 2027). Costco, Target and Lake Tapps nearby.
