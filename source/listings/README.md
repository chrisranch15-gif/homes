# Listing facts

One file per listing (`<street number>.json`). The listing page, seller portal, launch plan, signs and
social posts all read from it, so a fact is entered once.

## Listing input sheet (NWMLS Form 1 in TransactionDesk)

With Realist and the TransactionDesk input sheet open in Chrome, Claude copies public-record facts across
and saves them here. Copied from Realist (public record): subdivision/building name (from the legal
description), garage sq ft, sq ft and lot-size source, tax year and annual taxes, owner city/state,
occupant type, bank/REO, builder, style code, new construction, roof, heating, cooling, parking, basement,
HOA and common-interest community (when the legal description says condo), school district, middle and
high school.

Left for Chris (judgment or not in public record): price and dates, compensation, elementary school when the
district has more than one boundary, beds and baths by floor, exterior, energy source, sewer, appliances,
floor coverings, HOA dues and contact, remarks, showing instructions, condition.
