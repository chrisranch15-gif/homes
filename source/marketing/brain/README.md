# How the marketing brain learns

- `brain.md`: the strategy every marketing job reads first.
- `evidence.md`: what we've measured: Chris's best past posts and listings, top-performing hooks, client words,
  competitor study, platform guidance. Facts with sources and dates.
- `library/`: outside ideas we learned from (books, articles, posts Chris sends), summarized in our own words with
  a source link. Never paste a book or article in; never quote more than a short line.

## Teaching it something new (Chris)
Send it any of these and say "add this to the marketing brain":
- a link (article, post, video), a screenshot, a PDF, a voice note, or just an idea in your own words.
Three ways to send it:
1. In the Office or the Desk: **Teach the marketer** (bottom of the page). Paste the link or type the idea.
2. Tell the Concierge: "add this to the marketing brain: …".
3. In a chat with Claude.
It lands in the Sheet tab **Marketing brain inbox**. The next weekly review reads it, writes a short summary into
`library/` (in our own words, with the source), and proposes, in plain words, how it would change what we make
("Use this hook pattern on just-listed posts"), with a before/after example. You approve, and every marketing job
uses it from then on. Results tell us within a few weeks whether it actually helped.
