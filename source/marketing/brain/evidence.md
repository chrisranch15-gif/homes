- **Moments and formats that earn saves and shares:** price changes on a standout home, an agent-on-camera tour, a

## Competitor and platform study (deep study, Nov 26, 2026; details and links in library/)
- **Platforms reward sends, watch time and originality.** Instagram's top signals are watch time, likes per reach and
  sends per reach; since Apr 30, 2026 accounts that mostly repost others' work stop being recommended. Facebook
  demotes engagement bait ("tag 3 friends") and re-uploads. (library/platforms.md)
- **Formats:** Reels get 4x+ the interactions of single images and bring the most reach to accounts under 50K;
  carousels get ~9x the saves of single images; single-image engagement fell ~46% in a year. (library/luxury-accounts.md)
- **Real estate benchmark:** ~0.3% Instagram engagement per follower; benchmarked brands post about 3 Reels + 4
  carousels/images a week. Our 2026 posts run 5–13% of *reach* (a different measure; not comparable).
- **Leading luxury accounts:** agent on camera on a weekly rhythm; one listing re-cut into many pieces; tours of the
  home and its surroundings; market-insight posts; client stories. (library/luxury-accounts.md)
- **How clients choose agents:** mostly referral and past use (buyers 43% + 15%, sellers 37% + 29%); social works as
  proof for people who already know someone who knows us. (NAR 2025 profile)
- **Google Business Profile:** reviews and responding to them feed local prominence; phone numbers inside post text
  can get a post removed (use the Call button). **Google Search:** people-first, clear authorship; no special trick
  for AI Overviews; FAQ rich results ended May 2026. (library/platforms.md)
- **Compliance:** Washington requires the firm's full licensed name wherever a team name appears, and bars "real
  estate"/"realty" in a team name that isn't a licensed assumed name; HUD and Washington word-level Fair Housing risks
  added to the copy checker. (library/compliance.md)
