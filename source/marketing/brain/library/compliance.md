# Advertising rules we follow (our own summary; not legal advice)

Studied Nov 26, 2026 (deep study). Plain-words summary with sources. When in doubt, Chris asks his designated broker.
The copy checker (`source/checks/copy_rules.json`, rule `fair-housing`) enforces the clearest word-level risks.

## Fair Housing (federal)
- **The rule:** it's unlawful to publish any ad or statement about a home that shows a preference, limit or
  discrimination by race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin. Covers words,
  photos, symbols, and **where an ad is placed or who it's targeted to**.
  ([24 CFR 100.75, Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/100.75); read Nov 26, 2026)
- **The test (HUD 1995 advertising memo):** does the ad describe the *property* or the *people* wanted? Describe
  the home and what's nearby. ([Oregon Realtors summary](https://oregonrealtors.org/news/tip-of-the-week/permissible-advertisements-under-the-fair-housing-act/), May 3, 2024;
  [Texas A&M Real Estate Center](https://trerc.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/files/PDFs/Articles/1147.pdf), 1996)
- **Fine per that memo:** master bedroom, family room, mother-in-law suite, great view, walk-in closets, jogging
  trails, walking distance / walk to bus, quiet streets, desirable neighborhood, accessibility features (no-step entry,
  wheelchair ramp), "nonsmokers". (Many MLSs now prefer "primary bedroom"; HUD doesn't treat "master" as a violation.)
- **Risky (from the 1989 HUD word list and the memo):**
  race/color/origin labels (White, Black, Caucasian, Oriental, Hispanic…), religious labels or landmarks as a
  selling point (Christian home, near the church, Jewish/Catholic neighborhood); anything about who should live
  there by family status (adults only, no children, mature persons, singles, perfect for a young couple, empty
  nesters); disability as a person ("handicapped", "physically fit", "no wheelchairs"); one-sex housing; and code
  words in a selective context (restricted, exclusive, integrated, traditional). Lawful 55+ housing is an exception.
  ([1989 Part 109 text, govdocs mirror](https://corpora.tika.apache.org/base/docs/govdocs1/674/674405.pdf))
- **Photos:** models and people in marketing should reasonably reflect everyone; use the Equal Housing Opportunity
  logo/phrase where we can. (same source)
- **Digital ad targeting:** HUD's 2024 guidance on platform targeting was withdrawn (effective Sep 17, 2025;
  notice Apr 6, 2026), but the law itself still bans picking an audience that keeps protected groups from seeing a
  housing ad. Never narrow a paid housing ad by age, gender, family status or zip-code proxies.
  ([Federal Register 2026-06624](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-06624.pdf), Apr 6, 2026;
  [Orrick](https://infobytes.orrick.com/2026-04-10/hud-withdraws-eight-fair-housing-guidance-documents-as-part-of-deregulatory-review/), Apr 10, 2026)

## Fair Housing (Washington and Tacoma add more)
- Washington protects, beyond federal: marital status, sexual orientation (incl. gender identity), creed,
  citizenship or immigration status, families with children, honorably discharged veteran or military status,
  sensory/mental/physical disability, use of a dog guide or service animal. Ads may not directly or indirectly signal
  a limit. ([RCW 49.60.222](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/RCWPDF/RCW%20%2049%20%20TITLE/RCW%20%2049%20.%2060%20%20CHAPTER/RCW%20%2049%20.%2060%20.222.pdf), certified Jul 12, 2024)
- Tacoma adds age over 40, ancestry, and Section 8 / source of income (Tacoma Municipal Code 1.29).
  ([City of Tacoma](https://cityoftacoma.org/government/city_departments/equity_and_human_rights/fair_housing), read Nov 26, 2026)
- So: no "perfect for military families", "ideal for newlyweds", "no Section 8" in anything we write.

## Washington brokerage advertising (firm name, team name)
- **Every ad carries the firm's licensed name, clearly and conspicuously.** ([RCW 18.85.361(8)](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=18.85.361))
  Advertising includes websites, email, social media and internet ads.
  ([WAC 308-124B-210](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/WAC/WAC%20308%20%20TITLE/WAC%20308%20-124B%20CHAPTER/WAC%20308%20-124B-210.htm), effective Oct 31, 2010)
- **Team names:** a team brand that isn't a licensed assumed name may be used only if (a) the firm's licensed name
  shows clearly wherever the brand appears, (b) it doesn't sound like a separate company (no Inc, LLC, company,
  firm), (c) it **doesn't use words that usually mean a firm, such as "realty", "real estate", "realtors"**, and (d)
  the designated broker approved it in writing first. (same WAC)
- **DOL's advertising guidelines:** teams are not firms; a logo or web address alone doesn't satisfy the firm name;
  **a partial firm name isn't allowed** (so "KW" or "Keller Williams Realty PS" alone falls short); social media is
  advertising, with firm and broker names no more than one click away (we put them in the caption); every page of a
  site we control shows them; don't claim team or firm results as one person's own.
  ([DOL Real estate advertising guidelines](https://dol.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/real-estate-advertising-guidelines.pdf), RE-620-400, posted Mar 2023)
- **Open question for Chris (Nov 26, 2026):** "The Ranch Real Estate Group" contains "Real Estate". Unless it is a
  licensed assumed name of the firm (or the designated broker has confirmed it's allowed), WAC 308-124B-210(2)(c)
  appears to bar it. Confirm with the designated broker before any change; CLAUDE.md rule 7 stays as Chris wrote it.
- False or misleading statements are grounds for discipline (RCW 18.85.361(2)), which also covers altered or
  virtually staged photos shown without a label.

## NWMLS
- **First Look** status (from Sep 4, 2026): the listing is entered and visible to all members; public marketing is
  allowed up to 21 days before it goes Active; the seller chooses showings and IDX display.
  ([NWMLS](https://www.nwmls.com/northwest-mls-expands-listing-options/), Aug 31, 2026)
- Settlement-driven changes (secondhand): 60 days off-market before reusing First Look; portals show the listing
  broker's name and contact next to contact buttons (by Oct 15, 2026); NWMLS photo watermarks end.
  ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2026/08/31/compass-nwmls-settlement-rule-changes/), Aug 31, 2026)
- **Not public:** the NWMLS rulebook (advertising other members' listings, IDX display, photo rights, altered-image
  disclosure) is members-only. Until we have it: never advertise another firm's listing without that listing firm's
  permission and credit; label virtually staged or edited photos; AI video never goes to NWMLS (CLAUDE.md rule 8).
