# What top luxury agents and brokerages are doing (patterns, not copies)

Studied Nov 26, 2026 (deep study). Summaries in our own words. We learn the pattern and never borrow anyone's
branding, phrases, music or look.

## Patterns from the accounts
- **One listing, many pieces.** A big NYC team turned one listing into about nine assets (coming-soon still, trailer,
  virtual tour, feed stills, two short edits, long and short tours); the playful re-edit of the same footage did about
  3x the views of the first edit. Lesson: re-cut what we already shot before shooting more.
  ([Inman](https://inman.com/2022/04/29/9-pieces-of-content-from-1-listing-ryan-serhant-is-ready-to-teach-you-how), Apr 29, 2022, older but the method holds)
- **The agent on camera, every week, on a rhythm** (a "TV channel" with recurring segments), and answering comments
  and DMs personally. Small engaged audience over big passive one.
  ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/10/14/how-agents-like-ryan-serhant-build-businesses-on-social-media/), Oct 14, 2025;
  [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/best-real-estate-instagram-accounts/), Aug 12, 2025)
- **Brokerage brands with the best engagement** use short immersive tours of the home *and its surroundings*
  (Douglas Elliman, Sotheby's on TikTok, ~5% vs a 3.1% industry average) and scenic photography plus frequent market
  insight posts (Savills on Instagram, 0.9% vs 0.3% industry average).
  ([Dash Social real estate benchmark](https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2025/1H/real-estate-industry.pdf), data Jul–Dec 2024)
- **Team accounts that do well locally** mix just-sold proof, local market comparisons, and client stories and
  testimonials. ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/best-real-estate-instagram-accounts/), Aug 12, 2025)
- **Seattle-area agents getting real buyers from Instagram** do casual, personality-led home tours with commentary,
  unusual homes and local humor; one counts a handful of buyers a year from it, another calls tours her main lead
  source. ([Seattle Times via Spokesman-Review](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2025/aug/28/seattle-area-real-estate-agents-go-where-homebuyer), Aug 28, 2025)
  Caution: one leaves a home's downsides out to bait comments. That breaks our "every fact verified and fair" rule.
- **Long-form tours on YouTube** (home + town, "Town A vs Town B") are watched by luxury buyers on a TV.
  ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/best-real-estate-youtube-channels/), Sep 29, 2025)
- No published profile of a Gig Harbor / Pierce County luxury team's social strategy was found; a hand audit of 3–5
  local accounts would be the next step (unverified gap).

## Benchmarks worth knowing (definitions differ by vendor; don't compare across sources)
- **Formats (Instagram, all industries):** Reels get 4x+ the interactions of single images; carousels get about 9x the
  saves of single images; single-image engagement fell ~46% in a year; average Reel watch is ~8.5 seconds.
  ([Metricool 2026 Instagram study](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/), Jun 16, 2026)
- Engagement per follower: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%.
  ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-hashtag-study/), Feb 20, 2026)
- Under ~50K followers, Reels bring the most reach; carousels lead on engagement for bigger accounts.
  ([Inman roundup](https://www.inman.com/2026/07/06/latest-data-reveals-social-media-engagement/), Jul 6, 2026, secondhand)
- Real estate on Instagram: ~0.3% engagement, about 3 Reels + 4 carousels/images a week among benchmarked brands.
  ([Dash Social](https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2025/1H/real-estate-industry.pdf))
- Best general Instagram times: Tuesday–Wednesday afternoons; weekends weakest (all industries; test for us).
  ([Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/), Mar 31, 2026)

## How people actually choose an agent
- 39% of Realtors name social media as the tech that brings their best leads.
  ([HousingWire on NAR 2025 Technology Survey](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/nar-2025-technology-survey-realtor-tech-usage-trends/), Sep 18, 2025)
- But buyers mostly find agents by referral (43%) or past use (15%); sellers by referral (37%) or past use (29%).
  ([NowBam on NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers](https://nowbam.com/how-home-buyers-and-sellers-find-their-agents-in-2025/), Nov 17, 2025)
- Median repeat buyer is 62, median first-time buyer 40 (record highs).
  ([NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/news/economists-outlook/top-10-takeaways-from-nars-2025-profile-of-home-buyers-and-sellers), Nov 3, 2025)
- **What it means for us:** social is mostly proof and top-of-mind for people who already know someone who knows
  Chris. Posts that past clients and friends *send to someone* do the real work.

## Patterns that earn saves, shares and DMs (for agents)
- Carousels people can use later: market notes, checklists, town explainers ("Moving to Bonney Lake?").
- Agent-on-camera Reels, 20–60 seconds, strong first line, captions on screen.
- Before/after, local business picks, Q&A carousels, behind-the-scenes, Stories with polls.
  ([Seattle Agent](https://seattleagentmagazine.com/2026/09/09/the-instagram-strategy-todays-real-estate-agents-need/), Sep 9, 2026)
- "Comment a word and I'll send it" turns views into DM conversations (one agent attributes most clients to it).
  ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/10/14/how-agents-like-ryan-serhant-build-businesses-on-social-media/), Oct 14, 2025)
- Batch filming (one session every ~10 days, clips released every few days) fits a busy listing agent.
- Don't use the widely repeated "listings with video get 403% more inquiries": no traceable source.
