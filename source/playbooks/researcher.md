# Monthly researcher

Runs on the 2nd of each month. Also follow `locked-rules.md` and `improve.md`.
Google Sheet `1ROkc-18VbSn7kkLZYrTyJYiooMDLbBxYR0SSWItll2E`, tab **Research**
(Month | Idea | Where it came from | What it would do for you | What it would take | Chris's call | Note).

Goal: keep the Ranch system ahead. Bring Chris **at most one idea a month**, the one worth the most.

## Two places to look
1. **His own work (best ideas come from here).** Look for things Chris keeps doing by hand: repeated copy and
   paste (e.g. deal dates into KW Command), the same edits again and again (Edits tab), papercuts that keep
   coming back, sections he opens every day, questions sellers keep asking, steps a playbook says "Chris
   does this himself". Count how often in the last month.
2. **Outside.** Search for what's new and proven: new connectors for tools he uses (Gmail, Google, Metricool,
   KW Command, NWMLS, DocuSign/Authentisign, ShowingTime, Cloud CMA, Runway), new AI model or agent
   abilities, what top brokerages, luxury teams and engineering teams are publishing about agents and
   automation (like Uber's "How Uber Evals 1 Million Agent Skill Invocations a Month"). Prefer primary sources.
   Check NWMLS and Washington rules for anything that touches listings, advertising or AI use.

## Pick one
Score each candidate: time it saves Chris per month, client experience, risk, and effort. Pick the best.
Write one Research row, in plain words, phone-sized:
- **Idea:** one line.
- **Where it came from:** "your work" (with the count: "you copied deal dates into KW Command 9 times") or
  "outside" (with the source link).
- **What it would do for you:** concrete, with an estimate you can defend.
- **What it would take:** his time, any cost, anything he'd have to sign up for or approve, any risk.
Never sign up for anything, buy anything, connect anything or change any system from this task.

If an earlier row has Chris's call "Approve", note it in the weekly review's gather step so a build session
picks it up. Finish with a push notification: "Research: one idea for you this month: <idea>."
