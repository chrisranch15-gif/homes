# Social posts for listing moments

Read `source/marketing/brain/brain.md` first (the marketing strategy: the story, the moment, what to say). Also follow `locked-rules.md`, `improve.md` and the copy brief `source/marketing/social/README.md`.

Chris asked for this on 2026-11-25: "all the socials must be done up front, at least the just listed type of social graphics and captions... when we go pending, that should trigger the pending social designs and captions." A listing may go pending a day before the MLS shows it, so the contract itself is the trigger, not the MLS status. When the Office, the Desk or the TC fires this task, its message names the listing and the moment; handle that one first, then check the rest.

JOB: make sure every listing moment has its social set, ready for Chris to approve in the Office and the Desk.

1. Find the listings and their moment. Sources, earliest signal first: Google Sheet 1ROkc-18VbSn7kkLZYrTyJYiooMDLbBxYR0SSWItll2E tab "TC shadow" (the TC's log: any row whose text mentions mutual acceptance for a seller-side listing means pending, even before NWMLS changes; ignore rows about Discovery Park HOA board business), "Seller timeline" (Mutual acceptance / Closing day rows), "Sellers" (F = Pending/Closed), "Listings" (status column); then the public status on the listing page homes.ranchpnw.com/<key> and repo files source/listings/<key>.json and source/marketing/pieces/<key>.json (status, listed, contract, sold, sold_price). <key> is the listing page key (street number, e.g. 19917).
2. For each listing, the set it needs:
   - listed: status Active or Coming soon AND a listing page with real photos exists. If photos or facts are missing, skip it this run (it waits) and do not invent anything.
   - pending: any of the pending signals above. Use the listed date and the mutual acceptance date for the day count. If pieces/<key>.json lacks "contract", add the mutual date there (a verified fact from the contract) so the under-contract graphics and postcard rebuild too.
   - sold: Closed/Sold with a sold date; mention the price only if it is in the record and already public on the listing page.
   If source/marketing/social/<key>-<event>.json already exists, do nothing for that set.
3. Write source/marketing/social/<key>-<event>.json in the shape of source/marketing/social/19917-pending.json (posts ig, fb, story, gbp; layouts cover/statement/feature/list/close; formats feed/story/gbp). Write it like a master copywriter, per the brief and source/voice.md (if it exists): hook first, one idea per slide, specific not superlative, Chris's voice ("I"/"we"), never AI/system/automation, every fact verified from the listing record, no buyer/seller names, no offer counts. Look at the "Edits" tab rows with Kind "caption": follow what Chris changed before. Look at what performed (the weekly review notes the best posts in source/marketing/social/README.md under "What's working"). Just listed: cover with the home's strongest line, 2-3 feature or list slides with real rooms, a close inviting a private showing or a message. Pending: the timeline proof plus the reasons, then a close for future sellers. Sold: the result plus a close for future sellers. Instagram caption ends with "Chris Ranch · The Ranch Real Estate Group · (253) 335-5630" and the Keller Williams Realty Puget Sound line, then 4-6 local hashtags; Facebook no hashtags and one link (the listing page for listed, https://homes.ranchpnw.com/sell.html otherwise); Google under 1,500 characters, no hashtags, name and phone in the text; stories have no caption. Use only photos of that home; never put Holly's mountain photos (mtn-*) in a set whose close slide carries the Anthony Jones photo credit. Set "when" to the next good slot in America/Los_Angeles at least 18 hours out (weekday 6:30 PM or Saturday 9:30 AM); Chris can change it before he approves. Run the copy checker on every caption (`--kind caption`).
4. Build and look: python3 source/marketing/social.py <key> <event> all. Open every image you made and check it like a senior designer: no wrapped orphan words, nothing cut off, text clear of the photo's busy areas, story text inside the safe zones. Fix the copy or slide choices and rebuild until it is right.
5. Ship (rule 19, same session): bash source/office/ship.sh; commit and push homes and office (end commit messages with the attribution lines your environment gives); publish /home/claude/hub/index.html to the Office artifact ba66a063-8ba8-459c-8002-fc0ce149443d and /home/claude/app/index.html to the Desk artifact 6a12fcfd-7e65-4e8d-8acd-573532aab862 with publish_files (mode patch, base_version from get_artifact), including the new marketing/thumbs/soc-<key>-<event>-*.jpg files on BOTH artifacts.
