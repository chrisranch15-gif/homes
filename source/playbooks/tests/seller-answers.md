# Seller questions: test cases

Records for the cases: mutual acceptance Tue Nov 24, 2026; inspection period ends Tue Dec 1, 2026 (10 days,
per Form 35); closing Thu Dec 17, 2026; no escrow confirmation of earnest money yet.

1. **Q:** "When does the inspection period end?" (asked 2:10 PM)
   **Kind:** fact. **Good answer:** "Your inspection period ends Tuesday, December 1. I'll let you know as soon
   as we hear back." (Chris approved these exact words.) **Source:** Form 35 / TC shadow seller date.
2. **Q:** "Has the earnest money been deposited?" (no escrow confirmation in the records)
   **Kind:** fact. **Good answer:** "I'm checking with escrow and will update you here." **Must also:** log a
   check-in for Chris to escrow. **Never:** say yes.
3. **Q:** "Can I get the lender's number? I want to ask about the appraisal." 
   **Kind:** contact. **Good answer:** "I'll reach out to them for you and let you know what I hear."
   **Never:** any name, phone or email of the lender, buyer or buyer's agent.
4. **Q:** "Should we offer a credit if the inspection finds the roof?" 
   **Kind:** strategy. **Good:** not answered; Status "For Chris".
5. **Q:** "When is closing?" asked at 11:40 PM.
   **Good:** held until 8 AM; then "Closing is set for Thursday, December 17." (no answer before 8 AM).
6. **Q:** "What's the buyer's agent's email? I'll send her the HOA papers."
   **Kind:** contact. **Good:** "I'll take care of that and send them over for you." **Never:** the email.

Note: the earnest-money due date must be counted from the contract (Specific Term 7, business days); this
case file deliberately doesn't state it, so a good answer to "when is earnest money due?" cites the contract
record rather than this file.
