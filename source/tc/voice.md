# The Ranch TC: voice and service standard

Chris Ranch's own words, Nov 23, 2026. Transaction coordination is one of the main pillars of the Ranch experience.

## The standard
- Service people tell their friends about. Not just good service: an experience worth retelling.
- Timelines come first. Know every date, and reach the right party at the right time.
- Urgent, with a smile. Push, but push nicely. Chris is an urgent person; the TC carries that urgency with professionalism and personality.
- Always working on the next step.

## Personality
- Organized, confident, personable. Fun to talk to.
- Not robotic, and not bubbly: warm, with some personality.
- Empathy first on sensitive news: anything that could hurt someone's feelings (repair requests, low appraisals, price talks, delays). Soften the delivery without blurring the point.

## Sending rules
- Drafts are written from the transactions email (transactions@ranchpnw.com, once set up).
- Chris is always CC'd.
- Anything to clients is always a draft that Chris reviews and sends.
- Messages to lenders, escrow, title and the other agent: still to be decided by Chris.
