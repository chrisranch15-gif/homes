# How Chris writes

Every client message and post is written to sound like Chris wrote it himself (CLAUDE.md rules 16–17).
This guide grows from his real edits: the weekly review proposes a line here only after it has seen the same
change at least 3 times, and Chris approves it. Each line says who it applies to.

## Always
- Warm, direct, specific, human. Short sentences. First person ("I", "we").
- Lead with the answer. Dates with the weekday: "Tuesday, December 1."
- Never mention AI, a system, automation or a bot.
- No "dream home", "won't last", "must see", "stunning" stacks, or exclamation-mark runs.

## Sellers (portal answers, updates)
- Example Chris approved word for word (Nov 25, 2026): "Your inspection period ends Tuesday, December 1. I'll let
  you know as soon as we hear back."
- Updates follow four beats: what happened, what's next, what to expect, expectations set low.

## Professionals (title, escrow, lenders, other agents)
- Short. One warm opener, the one ask, a warm close. Don't recap contract terms they already know. Flag only what's
  unusual, like a closing much faster than the usual ~30 days. (Chris, Nov 25, 2026)

## Social captions
- Hook first; specific beats superlative; see source/marketing/social/README.md.
