Questions

Buying a home that isn't for sale.

What is an off-market home?

A home that changes hands without being listed on the MLS or the public home-search sites. Some owners would sell for the right buyer but never list, and some homes sell in the days before they would have gone live.

How do you find off-market homes around Gig Harbor and Puget Sound?

We start with a consult to get very clear on what you want, where, and how you're buying. Then we go hunting: through our network across 10 Keller Williams offices and, for By Invitation buyers, direct outreach to owners in the neighborhoods you choose. The details of how we do it stay between us and our clients.

Is it allowed to buy a home that isn't listed in Washington?

Yes. Owners can choose to sell privately, and we work within NWMLS rules on every Unlisted search.

Who is Unlisted for?

Buyers we represent who aren't already working with another agent. Inside Track is open at any price; By Invitation, which adds personal outreach to owners, starts at $750,000.

Where does Unlisted search?

Around Puget Sound, including Gig Harbor, Fox Island, Tacoma, Normandy Park, Federal Way, Bonney Lake, Lake Tapps and Enumclaw. Tell us where you want to live and we'll tell you how we'd approach it.

Does Unlisted still watch homes that come on the market?

Yes. We never stop watching the market for you. Sometimes the right home is found off market; sometimes it comes on the market, and because you've already seen what else is out there, you can say yes with confidence.
